## TCP connection open across a modem crash
AP: tcp_check.py discard 10.77.0.2 -n 33554432 (running); laptop 16:11:04 IST: zephyr-modem.sh stop + run net
AP 10:41:06 UTC (= 16:11:06 IST): ConnectionResetError: [Errno 104] Connection reset by peer
[AP] link reset: peer restarted (gen changed) (was UP)
[AP] link UP: peer id=28 gen=28, own gen=31 (recovery 47986 us)
  -> the restarted modem has no state for the connection and RSTs it: the AP app learns in
     ~2 s (a modem crash loses its sockets, as on real hardware). With the modem dead and NOT
     restarted, the same transfer instead failed with TimeoutError after the 30 s socket timeout.
